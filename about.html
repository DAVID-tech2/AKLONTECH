About AKLON

We're Building More Than Software

AKLON is a software development studio based in Kampala, Uganda. We engineer digital products that solve real problems — for businesses, institutions, and people with ambitious ideas.

Who We Are

A Studio That Cares About Both Sides

We care about the engineering — clean architecture, tested code, systems that hold up. And we care about the design — interfaces that feel right, experiences that make sense, details that show respect for the people using what we build.

That combination is rarer than it should be. Most teams lean one way or the other. We believe the best products come from treating both as non-negotiable. Every project we take on gets engineering rigor and design attention — not one or the other.

We work with businesses, educational institutions, healthcare providers, government agencies, and startups. The thread connecting them: they all need digital systems that work reliably and serve real people.

What Drives Us

Vision, Mission & Values

Vision

A future where technology serves everyone

We see a Uganda — and a continent — where digital systems are built locally, by people who understand the context, for the people who need them most. Technology shouldn't be imported. It should be built with, not just for.

Mission

Engineer digital products that create real impact

We turn ideas into deployed systems — and we keep them running. Every project we take on should make a measurable difference for the people and organizations it serves. That's the standard we hold ourselves to.

Values

Five principles, no exceptions

  • Innovation — new ideas, real solutions
  • Integrity — we mean what we say
  • Quality — in every line and pixel
  • Impact — measurable, meaningful
  • Partnership — we grow together
